# Paper Artifacts

This folder contains the result files, scripts, tables, and figures used in the paper.

## Model combination

Everything related to model combination, including the strategy comparison, prompt-guidance ablation, Shetland and Police Scotland results, and single-hypothesis post-editing, is in:

`01_model_combination/`

The paper-facing tables are the `.txt` files inside `01_model_combination/tables/`. The corresponding scripts, result data, and figures are in the neighbouring folders.

## Sentence-level confidence

Everything related to sentence-level confidence is in:

`02_sentence_confidence/`

The main paper-facing results are the `.txt` files inside `02_sentence_confidence/tables/`. Calibration results are inside `02_sentence_confidence/calibration/`. The scripts, source result files, combined figures, and separate dataset figures are in their respective folders.

The human-validation results will be added after the participant analysis is complete.
